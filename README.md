# Buana Studio Direct — Print Cover Calculator & Multi-Imprint Template Engine

A professional, print-ready book cover dimension calculator and dynamic template generator inspired by Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP), enhanced with **multi-brand imprint management** and audience segment tailoring.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Multi-Brand Imprint Management & Segment Adaptation
- **FALAH BOOKS**: Dedicated for readers **Below 13 yo** (Children, Illustrated Picture Books, Middle Grade, Workbooks). Preconfigured with square (8.5" x 8.5") / letter (8.5" x 11") presets, premium color profiles, and cheerful brand badges.
- **BAYAN PRESS**: Dedicated for readers **Above 13 yo** (Young Adult, Adult Fiction, Non-Fiction, Literary, Academic Treatises). Preconfigured with Trade standard (5.5" x 8.5", 6" x 9"), cream paper profiles, and sleek typography.
- **BUANA HERITAGE & Custom Imprints**: Add, edit, and persist any custom publishing brand imprint with tailored age demographics, ISBN prefixes, and branding colors.

### 2. High-Precision Cover Calculation Engine
- **Binding Types**: Paperback (Perfect bound) and Hardcover (Case laminate with 0.59" wrap and 0.394" hinge allowance).
- **Interior & Paper Caliper PPI**: Exact calculations for White Paper (444 PPI), Cream Paper (400 PPI), and Premium Color (426 PPI).
- **Spine Safety & Minimum Page Warnings**: Real-time validation for spine text eligibility (minimum 0.0625" / ~79 pages).
- **Bleed & Margins**: Industry standard 0.125" (3.175 mm) bleed and interior safe margin boundaries.
- **Orientation**: Full support for both Left-to-Right (LTR) and Right-to-Left (RTL / Arabic / Hebrew / Manga).

### 3. Interactive Multi-Mode Cover Preview
- **Guide Grid View**: Color-coded schematic showing Bleed (pink), Trim lines (black), Safe margin (cyan), Spine folds, and Barcode zone.
- **Flat 2D Artwork Mockup**: Clean jacket visualization with imprint styling.
- **3D Perspective Book View**: Realistic 3D simulated rendering with spine thickness.

### 4. Multi-Format Instant Template Downloads
- **Print-Ready Vector PDF**: Vector lines with exact millimeter / inch coordinates for InDesign, Illustrator & Affinity.
- **High-Resolution PNG (300 DPI)**: Crisp raster overlay for Canva and Adobe Photoshop.
- **Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG)**: Layered SVG for Figma and web design tools.
- **JSON Specification File**: Machine-readable metadata package.
